# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Businesses and entrepreneurs looking to hire a premium digital agency for high-end web design and development. They visit to evaluate Sevora Lab's technical expertise, design quality, and to request a project quote.

## Product Purpose
A premium showcase for Sevora Lab, a premier web development and digital studio in Sri Lanka specializing in high-performance websites, custom web apps, and creative branding solutions. Success is measured by generating high-quality client inquiries and requests for quotes.

## Brand Personality
Premium, cutting-edge, and professional. The brand blends technical mastery (interactive 3D, immersive layouts) with reliable, high-end execution.

## Anti-references
Generic, boring SaaS templates. Avoid warm beige/cream background defaults, repetitive/identical card grids, and side-stripe borders. Also avoid typical generic developer agency portfolios that lack interactive polish.

## Design Principles
- **Aesthetic Mastery**: Show, don't tell. Leverage interactive 3D, premium typography, and custom glow effects to visually prove technical capability.
- **Clear Affordances**: Guide users directly to the primary action (starting a project or requesting a quote) with high-contrast, clear, and descriptive interactive controls.
- **Customized Premium Feel**: Steer clear of standard SaaS templates and generic Tailwind patterns. Colors, shadows, and spacing should feel carefully custom-crafted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
General WCAG AA compliance. Ensure text has sufficient contrast against background fills. Respect user preferences by supporting `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` for all transitions.
